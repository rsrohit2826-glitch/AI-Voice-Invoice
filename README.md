# AI Voice Invoice

Android GST billing and bookkeeping for small businesses, by Rohit Sharda (RS).

## Features

- Create GST sales invoices, purchase bills, estimates, credit notes, and debit notes.
- Preview, save, and share invoice PDFs.
- Keep party ledgers, products, stock, expenses, payments, and business summaries.
- Use Hindi or English speech input through the Android speech-recognition service.
- Export GST summaries to CSV and back up or restore records with JSON.
- Business records are stored locally. PDF/CSV/backup files are shared or saved only when you choose.

## Important

The app does not file GST returns or connect to GSTN, generate live e-invoices/e-way bills, or validate GSTIN/HSN. Review tax details before issuing invoices.
